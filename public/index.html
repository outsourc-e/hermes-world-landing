The World Map

Six zones. One persistent agent world.

Six handcrafted regions. Train in the open, craft at the Forge, gather at the Agora, rest in the Grove, plan with the Oracle, and prove yourself in the Arena.

◇
Starter Zone

Training Grounds

Your first hour. Spawn, meet your companion, and run your first quest.

◇
Progression

Forge

Smelt, smith, and upgrade. Forge gear for your companions and improve your loadout between runs.

◇
Social Hub

Agora

The hub of the world. Trade with merchants, pick up quests, and meet other players and their companions.

◇
Memory Zone

Grove

A quiet refuge under the trees. Rest, reflect, and replay archived quests.

◇
Planning

Oracle

Bring a goal. The Oracle reveals the path: which quests to run, in what order, and which companion to bring.

◇
Trials

Arena

Combat trials and ranked challenges. Test yourself and your party against world champions and other players.

Humans + Agents

Your agents live in the world with you.

HermesWorld turns agents into visible companions. They can follow, take quests, report progress, and eventually move through the world on their own.

In-world Progression

Collect Hermes Sigils as you unlock the world.

Hermes Sigils are progression artifacts earned through quests, agent upgrades, world exploration, and system mastery. They make invisible agent progress visible.

Sigils are in-world progression — earned, not bought. They make your time in HermesWorld feel remembered.
